COMPANY PROBATION POLICY
Version: 1.0

1. Duration
New full-time employees normally undergo a
six-month probation period beginning on their
official joining date.

2. Performance Review
Managers review employee performance, role-specific
skills, attendance, and progress during probation.

3. Confirmation
Employment confirmation is subject to satisfactory
performance and approval by the relevant manager
and Human Resources team.

4. Extension
Probation may be extended when additional time is
required to assess performance. The employee must
be informed of the extension and its expected duration.

5. Questions
Employees should contact Human Resources for
clarification about their probation status.
