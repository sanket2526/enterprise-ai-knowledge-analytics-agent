COMPANY REMOTE WORK POLICY
Version: 1.0

1. Eligibility
Employees may be eligible for up to two remote
working days per week, subject to role requirements
and manager approval.

2. Availability
Employees working remotely must remain reachable
during their team's agreed working hours.

3. Data Security
Employees must use company-approved devices and
follow company information security guidelines.
Confidential company data must not be shared
through unauthorized services.

4. Approval
Remote work arrangements must be agreed upon with
the employee's manager. Business requirements
may affect remote work availability.

5. Meetings
Employees are expected to attend scheduled meetings
and communicate their availability to teammates.
