COMPANY LEAVE POLICY
Version: 1.0

1. Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 20 days of annual leave
per calendar year. Annual leave is credited monthly.

2. Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of sick leave per year.
A medical certificate may be requested for absences
exceeding three consecutive working days.

3. Leave Approval
Employees should submit leave requests at least
five working days before the planned leave.
Managers are responsible for reviewing requests.

4. Carry Forward
A maximum of five unused annual leave days may be
carried forward to the next calendar year, subject
to company policy.

5. Emergency Leave
Employees should inform their manager as soon as
possible when an emergency prevents advance notice.
